# Maintain Logs Rule

When working in this repository, you MUST always maintain the `ACTION_LOG.md` and `CHANGES_LOG.md` files located in the root of the workspace.

1. **ACTION_LOG.md**: Update this file with a summary of the high-level actions and decisions taken during your task.
2. **CHANGES_LOG.md**: Update this file with a record of the specific files changed, including a brief description of what was added, modified, or removed.

Before concluding your task or handing back to the user, ensure both logs have been appropriately updated to reflect the work you just performed.
